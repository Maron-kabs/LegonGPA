# Legon GPA
 
A CGPA calculator for University of Ghana students, built on the official UG 4.0 grading scale.
 
Add your courses with their credit hours and grades to get your semester GPA, or enter your existing CGPA and credits to work out your new cumulative CGPA. It shows your degree classification band as you go, and saves your courses on your device.
 
## Features
 
- Calculate this semester's GPA from your courses
- Combine with a previous CGPA to get your new cumulative CGPA
- Live degree classification (First Class, Second Upper, and so on)
- Saves your data locally in the browser
- Built on the official UG grading scale
## UG grading scale
 
| Grade | Marks | Grade Point |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| A | 80–100 | 4.0 |
| B+ | 75–79 | 3.5 |
| B | 70–74 | 3.0 |
| C+ | 65–69 | 2.5 |
| C | 60–64 | 2.0 |
| D+ | 55–59 | 1.5 |
| D | 50–54 | 1.0 |
| E | 45–49 | 0.5 |
| F | 0–44 | 0.0 |
 
CGPA = total grade points ÷ total credits, where a course's grade points = credits × grade point.
 
## A note on your final class
 
This calculates the equal-weighted CGPA you track each semester. Your **final** class of degree is decided by the FGPA, which weights the four levels in a 1:1:2:2 ratio (L100 : L200 : L300 : L400), so third and fourth year count more. Always confirm against your official transcript.
 
## Run it
 
Open `gpa.html` in any web browser. No setup needed.
 
## Built with
 
Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks.
