Good to know: This calculates the equal-weighted CGPA (total grade points ÷ total credits) — the figure you track each semester. Your final class of degree is decided by the FGPA, which weights the four levels in a 1:1:2:2 ratio (L100 : L200 : L300 : L400), so your final result leans more on your third and fourth year.
